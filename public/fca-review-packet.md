# Future Citizen Authority Review Packet

This packet is a lightweight first-meeting brief for teams evaluating a controlled Future Citizen Authority pilot. It is intended for scoping, risk review, and internal circulation before production data or partner-specific records are connected.

Contact: review@fca.ms

## Pilot scope memo

Define one useful workflow before evaluating the wider platform.

- Responsible owner and decision sponsor
- Eligible participant group or operating team
- First service, treasury, or identity workflow
- Success metric for a 60-90 day pilot
- Reporting cadence and scale, pause, or revise decision point

## Evidence files to prepare

- Identity credential model: issuer authority, credential schema, proof type, revocation path, holder lifecycle
- Custody boundary: MPC roles, recovery council, device controls, signing limits, escalation procedure
- Approval matrix: action class, requester, approver, quorum, audit event, emergency override
- Audit trail map: issuance event, approval event, settlement event, revocation event, reporting export
- Data boundary note: raw records, proof outputs, public references, retention policy, access logs

## Review sequence

1. Briefing: confirm the first workflow and accountable owner.
2. Control review: accept identity, custody, approval, and data boundaries for pilot.
3. Pilot authorization: approve operators, success metrics, reporting cadence, and escalation path.
4. Scale decision: review evidence from the pilot window and decide whether to scale, pause, or revise.
